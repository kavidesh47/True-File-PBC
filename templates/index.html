About Truefile

A wax seal for the digital age.

TRUEFILE is a private blockchain-based document verification system that helps users verify whether a digital document is original or tampered.

Original documents are securely fingerprinted using SHA-256 hashing and recorded in a private blockchain ledger. Users can upload their documents and instantly compare them with the stored blockchain record..
